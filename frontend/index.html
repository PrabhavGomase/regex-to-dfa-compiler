Literals, union |, grouping, * + ?, and escaped punctuation. Spaces are literals. Empty regex or () accepts the empty string.

The lexer identifies literals and operators with zero-based source offsets. Escaped punctuation becomes one literal token.

    The parser applies postfix repetition first, then concatenation, then union. Unary nodes wrap one child; binary nodes combine two.

    Thompson construction turns each AST node into a fragment. Dashed ε edges consume no input. Double circles are accepting states; the filled state is the start.

    Each DFA state is an ε-closed set of NFA states. On a symbol, move to its destinations and take ε-closure again. Missing transitions reject.

    Moore refinement separates states whose future acceptance differs. Unreachable states disappear; missing edges share a rejecting sink before refinement. This DFA is complete over its alphabet.

    The position method skips the NFA. States contain positions that may occur next. The dedicated end marker determines acceptance and is distinct from a literal #.
